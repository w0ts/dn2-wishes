The hidden functions of DN2wishes on a Digitone II: key combinations, held keys and behaviour the screen does not show, one card each. What the menus and lists show (the new machines, the inserts, their knobs) is not repeated here. Unofficial: not made or supported by Elektron.

The drawings show the keys a function uses, not the instrument's layout. Each card was checked against the firmware's code.
