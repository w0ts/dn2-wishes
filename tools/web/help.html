WISHES · HIDDEN FUNCTIONS

The hidden functions of DN2wishes v0.56.1 on a Digitone II: key combinations, held keys and behaviour the screen does not show, one card each. What the menus and lists show (the new machines, the inserts, their knobs) is not repeated here. Unofficial: not made or supported by Elektron.

The drawings are a schematic of the keys a function uses, not the instrument's layout. Cards marked with a part apply only to builds that include it (?off=INS_FUZZ,FX_XDLY,... hides the parts a build leaves out). Written from the firmware's docs/HIDDEN-FUNCTIONS.md; items marked "unverified" were read from notes or code, not confirmed on a unit.
