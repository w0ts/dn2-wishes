Digitone II sounds (.dn2pst) for the machines Wishes adds: an INIT and a set of sounds for each machine of ours, and an INIT for each of the other machines. One zip holds them all. The same files are in the repository's presets folder.

Wishes only Our machines are saved by their machine type (JUNO 50, ANALOG 2 51, DRONE 52, SOPHIE 53, TB-303 54, PLAITS 55, MACHINEDRUM 5, MONOMACHINE 6), so these sounds need a Wishes build made since 2026-11-18: the release on the builder (v0.62.2 or later). On an older build, or on stock firmware, they load as other machines or as silent ones. Unofficial: not made or supported by Elektron.

How to load them

  1. Back up your +Drive with Elektron Transfer.
  2. Unzip the download.
  3. In Transfer, with the Digitone II connected over USB, send the .dn2pst files to a bank of its +Drive presets (a free bank keeps your own sounds apart).
  4. On the Digitone II, load one onto a track as any other preset.
