Digitone II Mods / sample transfer

Sample Transfer

Send your own samples to a Digitone II running the sampler mod: into the +Drive library, or straight into a project's SAMP slots. Pick the instrument's MIDI ports, drop WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, OGG or M4A files, see how much memory they take, and send them over USB.

This page talks to your instrument. It sends samples as SysEx over USB to a Digitone II that already runs the sampler mod (v0.22 or later; the +Drive library: v0.31 or later). Your audio files are read in this tab and go only to the instrument; there is no server to upload them to.

Before you start. Chrome or Edge (Web MIDI with SysEx; allow it when asked). On the instrument, USB CONFIG set to USB MIDI or Overbridge. Close Elektron Transfer first. Back up your +Drive: sending to the project slots replaces the samples you sent before.

Connect to see the instrument.

Select samples on the left and press Send, or drag them onto the right; audio files dropped straight onto the +Drive go into the folder that is open. Names: the instrument's list shows the whole name (up to 35 characters); the seven letters under it are its short name. A dropped folder's samples get its name as their folder, and its folders stay folders inside it (v0.40 or later on the instrument; six levels at most, deeper files go into the sixth; before v0.40, each path is one folder). Ticked folders are deleted with their samples (v0.35 or later on the instrument; before that, empty folders go on the instrument: +DRIVE, RIGHT). Moving and renaming: on the instrument.

Firmware

The mod itself (the sample machines, the recorder, PERC and the rest) is a Digitone II OS 1.11 update file, DN2mod_vX.Y_NAME_OS1.11.syx. Every build is checked by the project's ship gate before it is published, and only that exact file (its sha256) is cleared. Flash it as any Digitone II OS update (Elektron Transfer, or the instrument's SysEx receive), after backing up the +Drive. The DN2 Mod Patcher builds it in your browser from your own copies of Elektron's stock OS files (Digitone II 1.11, Digitakt II 1.17, Machinedrum UW 1.63), and lets you save it only if it is byte for byte the cleared file. Each version's notes and tests: FIRMWARE.md.

6

Diagnostics

For bug reports: asks the instrument what its DSP has seen (needs v0.34 or later, connected above). It only reads. Run it right after power-on, again right after a note goes silent, and once more after a minute with nothing playing; press Copy each time and paste the results into your report.

Erase mod data

  

    
Before going back to stock firmware (needs v0.39 or later, connected above): erases everything the mod keeps on the +Drive (the samples in the pool, the projects' sample lists, the +Drive library, the mod's settings) and nothing of Elektron's (projects, sounds, presets are untouched). Then restart the instrument. The same is on the instrument: in the sample browser, +DRIVE, RIGHT, ERASE MOD DATA.
