# LIVE SELECT

2026-11-12. `csrc/msel/msel.c` (the behaviour), `csrc/msel/live.c` + `live.S` (the setting's row),
`csrc/jump/jump.c` (the settings sector's flags), `test/test_live_select.py`.

The User: *"why do we need YES for the engine to load/preview, and YES again to confirm? Can we have a
preference: preview/load the engine on the arrows, confirm and exit on YES, discard and revert on NO?
(same for filters)"*.

## What the User gets

**SETTINGS > PERSONALIZE** (stock's preferences, beside SYSTEM, whose rows are only submenus) has a last
row, **LIVE SELECT**, with stock's own toggle box. YES,
LEFT/RIGHT or the knob change it. It is **on** by default: it is kept in the mod's settings sector
(`0x6FF000`, DIRECT JUMP's) as flag bit 2 = *off* (bit 1 is REMOTE CONTROL's, usb-config), so a unit without the sector, or with an older
sector, has it on.

While it is on:

- **MACHINE SEL**, both pages (machines and filters): the machine (or filter) under the cursor is put on
  the track once the cursor has rested 250 ms (30 ticks of the panel tick, 120.0 a second on the unit; it was 150 ticks, 1.25 s, until 2026-11-14:
  measured on the unit on r5, key to commit 1.32 s +-3 ms over 5 tries). Arrows and the knob both count.
  - YES commits anything still pending, then closes (stock's YES on the track's own row).
  - NO puts back the machine and the filter the track had when MACHINE SEL opened, then the whole sound
    (1,163 bytes) as it was, and closes.
- **FUNC + G filter list** (the FLTR page): the same. YES, pushing G or letting go of FUNC keeps the
  filter, and NO reverts. Another key or knob closes the list and keeps what is previewed.
- Off: stock's MACHINE SEL (YES commits, NO closes) and the list as before.

## Why NO restores the whole sound

Measured on stock 1.12 in the emulator: a machine commit (`0x40031880`, the commit stock's YES makes,
about 9,000 ColdFire instructions) resets the machine's parameter block to its defaults. That is sound
+70..+128 (28 bytes changed, among them a knob just turned). Committing the old machine back brings its
defaults, not the User's values (23 bytes still differed). So NO first puts the types back through
stock's commits (the voice gets its machine and filter as stock gives them), then copies the saved
sound over the track's sound. A filter commit (`0x40031a56`) changes only the filter type (sound +0xDF);
the filter's parameters stay (measured: MULTI-MODE → LOWPASS 4). The same save/restore covers it.

## Measured in the 1.12 emulator

The build is the integrator's regenerated series `rr-v0.48-ours-1.12` (cfbb41a0) plus these commits;
the build's own snapshot. Proof sheet: `live-select-proof.png` in the session notes.

| PERSONALIZE | LIVE SELECT is its last row, ticked (on) |

## Not verified

  just more often. **Unit check, logged for later:** play a held note and a running pattern while

- A voice already sounding when NO restores the sound keeps what it had until its next note (the

## AMP ON/OFF on the drum machines (2026-11-20)

`csrc/msel/ampsw.c` (the page, the field, the mask), `src/dnfw/waverider/ampsw.py` (the table, the DSP's hooks),
`csrc/waverider/sharc/ampsw.asm` (generated by `scripts/gen_ampsw_asm.py`), `csrc/sampler/link.c` (the word to the DSP);
gate steps `drumamp` (`scripts/emu_amp_switch.py`, ColdFire) and `ampdsp` (`scripts/sharc_ampsw_step.py`, SHARC runner);
`test/test_amp_switch.py`. The User's decisions (2026-11-20, final), after the audit of stock's AMP on our drum machines
(stock's default AMP fades a hit's first 2 ms in, and its release cuts every tail at the trig's end). It replaces the
first approach of the same day (the AMP records set fully open at a pick, the AMP from before put back on leaving).

**What the User gets.** On stock's AMP page (ENV1/AMP, 1/2), **VOL's place (H) is AMP ON/OFF** while the track's
machine is a drum machine. **OFF is the default**: the voice truly bypasses stock's AMP envelope **and VOL** in the
DSP: the drum leaves the chain exactly as it reached the AMP stage, gain 1.0 bit for bit; the AMP knobs and VOL do not
apply (they keep their values: ON plays them again). **ON** is stock's AMP and VOL exactly as usual. H turned right: ON,
left: OFF; H pushed: the other one. Every other machine keeps VOL on H exactly as stock. The drum machines (one table,
`dnfw.waverider.ampsw`, by machine name, so a later one joins by name):

| machine | AMP ON/OFF |
|---|---|
| MACHINEDRUM (5) | always |
| SOPHIE (53) | always |
| DRUM (56) | always, every TYPE |
| PLAITS (55) | while MODL is on BD, SD or HH (MODL values 78..100: the DSP's choice, `plaits.dsp_model`); on any other model VOL as stock |

**Stored per sound**, in the sound's wishes record (`WT_AMP_SW` 0x0014, one byte, 1 = ON; `docs/wishes-record.md`).
OFF is no field at all: every old sound, preset and project, and every new pick, reads OFF on a drum machine. The field
travels with the sound as the record does (copy, paste, SAVE, LOAD, presets, kits); it is not tied to the machine, so a
sound set ON keeps ON when it moves to another drum machine.

**How the DSP bypasses it** (Elektron's per-slot chain, sw 0x1c8ef1; stock's own code runs untouched):

- The ColdFire (`as_poll`, every UI pass, recomputed only when the kit, a track's machine or MODL word, or a record
  moved) keeps a 16-bit mask: bit t = track t's sound is a drum machine with AMP OFF. `link.c` sends `MAGIC | mask` to
  the pool's READY page (`ampsw.pool_off()`, the word after the mixer meters' LV_WANT) at each change, three times
  (one may be lost), and again every 2 s while a bit is set; after the READY page (`smp_tables` 2), which K_READY
  zeroes at every boot.
- Hook A (sw 0x1c99b4, before the per-slot AMP loop sw 0x1c99bf): for each voice slot whose track (the slot map ROUTE,
  as the same block's frame carries it: no lag at a note-on) has its bit, the chain's buffer table entry is pointed
  at a scratch buffer of ours. Stock's AMP stage (sw 0xb80345) and VOL's loop (sw 0x1c99ed, (VOL / 32768)^2 ramped)
  then run as stock, on the scratch: their state (the envelope, VOL's ramp) moves on exactly as stock's, so ON later
  continues from stock's own state. Hook B (sw 0x1c9a3a, after VOL's loop) puts the table back from its copy (the
  output stage's 0x257ea8, the same 16 addresses: engine init copies it, trackbuf.asm writes both).
- Cost: a word read, a compare and a branch a block with no drum OFF (about 25 instructions for both hooks); with one,
  about 10 more a slot. The PERC rest still works: it reads the voice's buffer after the chain, now the drum's own
  decay.
- Room: ampsw.asm 346 B in L2's gap after PI-RS's table (0x200A9C90..0x200AA000), its data 192 B in L1 F2's tail
  (0x2E1B40..0x2E1C00): claimed in `test/test_dsp_claims.py`.
- **Elektron's DSP code rule**: declared in `scripts/sharc_stockout_step.py` (the User's option 1): output words may
  differ from stock only for slots playing a track whose bit is set, and only while it is. With stock machines no bit
  is ever set, so `stockout` stays bit-identical.

**Known limit.** The bit is the track's: a voice playing another sound (a sound lock) follows its track's setting and
its track's drum test. A lock to a synth sound on a drum track with AMP OFF bypasses that synth's AMP too.

**Checked.** `ampdsp` (the SHARC runner, this tree's DSP image, SOPHIE on slot 0 and WaveTone on slot 1, ATK 16 and VOL
64 on both): track 0 OFF: slot 0 leaves VOL's loop bit for bit as it reached the AMP stage, every block, while stock's
AMP level of that slot moves on as in the run without the word, and every other slot is the same as there; track 0 ON
with every other bit set: slot 0 bit-identical; a word without MAGIC: every slot and the main mix bit-identical.
`drumamp` (the ColdFire emulator, the build's own snapshot): every machine picked from an odd AMP and VOL: the AMP words
and VOL never written; the mask's bit exactly on the drum machines (PLAITS: MODL onto BD SD VA HH: on on off on); the
field set and cleared; the page drawn, H turned right / left / pushed, VOL untouched; H on WaveTone turns VOL as stock;
stock's SAVE / LOAD carrying ON, an old stored sound reading OFF.

### Voices with AMP OFF: do they still rest? (measured for the first approach, the same gain)

The audit's open point. Three measurements (session 2026-11-20):

(Measured 2026-11-20 with the first approach's AMP fully open, a gain of exactly 1.0 like OFF's; its two scripts,
`scripts/emu_drumamp_alloc.py` and `scripts/drumamp_rest.py`, were removed with it: commit 4afd7f82 / 9acf4660.)

- **Stock's allocator and the VOICE BUDGET never read the AMP** (`scripts/emu_drumamp_alloc.py`, stock 1.12's
  allocator in the emulator, the paravoice rig): 32 short notes on one track, 15 keys held and one more, the budget
  stealing among sounding release tails, each with every track's AMP at stock's default and fully open: the same
  slots and the allocator's memory byte for byte. A released voice is free to stock's allocator at its key-up; the
  budget steals sounding release tails first. A voice is never held back from a new note by its AMP.
- **The DSP's rest** (PERC: a voice rests after 64 blocks under -96 dB with no note, `dnfw.perc.program`): with the
  AMP open only the machine's own decay ends a voice (`scripts/drumamp_rest.py`, the run-mode model, one hit, at
  most 12 s). See the table below.
- What a voice that never rests costs: it renders (and is heard) until its slot gets another note, as a stock
  sound with REL INF does. Before this change stock's REL ended it at the trig's end.

Measured 2026-11-20 (one hit, at most 12 s; 86 of 91 cases rest):

| machine | at its defaults | at the top of its controls |
|---|---|---|
| MACHINEDRUM, every machine | every one rests; the longest GND-SN 6.4 s, TRX-CY 2.4 s, PI-BD 1.3 s, the rest under 1 s | most rest within 10.1 s (TRX-BD); **never**: TRX-B2 (still -10.5 dB at 12 s), GND-SN (-17.9 dB), GND-NS (-5.4 dB: noise), TRX-OH (-67 dB, still falling) |
| SOPHIE, each model | 0.33..0.35 s | DCAY at its top: 3.7..3.9 s |
| PLAITS BD / SD / HH | HARM TIMB MORP 64: 1.28 / 0.92 / 1.00 s; at 0: 0.38 / 0.22 / 0.12 s | at 127: BD **never** (-82.7 dB at 12 s), SD 5.1 s, HH 9.6 s |

So a machine set to hold (the Machinedrum's DEC at its top, PLAITS' BD at its longest) rings until its voice's slot
is given to another note. **Kept so** (the User, 2026-11-20): those machines have their own decay, and at its top it
is meant to sustain; AMP ON brings stock's AMP back for that sound.
